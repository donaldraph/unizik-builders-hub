## Section 2: Data Collection Toolkit

How to capture every metric cleanly during the event.

### 1. Attendance Tracking

**Setup (1 week before):**
- Create a Google Form with fields: Full Name, Email, University, Department, Year, Phone Number, "How did you hear about this event?"
- Generate a QR code linking to the form
- Print 2 copies of the QR code poster (A4 minimum) for the check-in desk
- Prepare a physical backup sign-in sheet (name, email, phone) for connectivity issues

**Day-of execution:**
- Station 2 volunteers at the check-in desk
- Every attendee scans the QR code or fills the physical sheet — no exceptions
- For walk-ins, add a "walk-in" tag in the form or a separate section on the sheet
- At the 1-hour mark, export the form count and note it as "early attendance"
- At event close, export final form responses immediately

**Tip:** Have a separate internal headcount done by a volunteer doing a physical sweep of the venue at peak session time. This catches people who skipped check-in.

### 2. Social Media Tracking

**Setup (1 week before):**
- Define the official hashtag: **#AWSStudentBuilderGroup** (mandatory) plus an event-specific tag like **#AWSreInventAwka** or **#AWSCommunityDayAwka**
- Display the hashtag on every slide, banner, and name badge
- Assign 1 volunteer as the Social Media Tracker

**Day-of execution:**
- The Social Media Tracker searches the hashtag on Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok every 2 hours
- Screenshot the search results and note the count
- At event close, screenshot the total post count and top posts
- Within 24 hours, compile:
  - Total posts per platform
  - Total impressions (available on X Analytics, Instagram Insights, LinkedIn post analytics)
  - Top 5 posts by engagement
  - Any posts from sponsors or notable accounts

**Tool option:** Use a free social listening tool like Brand24 trial or manually compile via native search.

### 3. App Downloads & Sponsor Registrations

**Setup (2 weeks before):**
- Contact each sponsor who wants to track sign-ups
- Request one of the following from them:
  - A unique **referral link** or **UTM-tagged URL** for the event
  - A unique **promo code** (e.g., `UNIZIKBUILD26`) that students enter on sign-up
  - A **QR code** pointing to a tracked landing page
- Test every tracking link before the event

**Day-of execution:**
- Display sponsor QR codes / promo codes on slides during their speaking slot
- Include them on printed handouts at sponsor booths
- Have booth volunteers remind attendees to use the specific code
- Sponsors pull their own analytics post-event — follow up with them on Day 2

### 4. AWS Builder Center Sign-Ups

**How it works:**
- AWS provides every Student Builder Group Leader a **unique Builder Center sign-up link**
- Share this link via QR code at check-in, on slides, and in follow-up emails
- AWS tracks the number of accounts created through your link — you don't need to do anything extra
- Request the count from your Account Manager within 1 week post-event, or check your Leader dashboard

**Day-of execution:**
- Print the QR code on a large poster at the check-in desk
- Display it on a slide at the start and end of every session
- Have the MC remind attendees 2-3 times during the event

### 5. Photo & Video Documentation

**Setup (1 week before):**
- Designate 1 official photographer (camera or high-quality phone)
- Designate 1 person for short-form video (Instagram Reels, TikTok)
- Create a shot list:
  - [ ] Venue exterior before attendees arrive
  - [ ] Check-in desk with queue
  - [ ] Crowd wide shot at peak attendance
  - [ ] Each speaker on stage
  - [ ] Every sponsor banner clearly visible and readable
  - [ ] Booth interactions (sponsor rep talking to students)
  - [ ] Game activities in action (Card Clash, Syntax Sprint)
  - [ ] Workshop hands-on moment (laptops open, students building)
  - [ ] Winner celebrations and prize distribution
  - [ ] Group photo (all attendees)
  - [ ] Candid networking moments
  - [ ] Food / refreshment setup (shows event production value)

**Day-of execution:**
- Photographer follows the shot list throughout the event
- Video person captures 3-5 short clips (15-60 seconds each) for reels
- Collect SD card or receive AirDrop/transfer within 2 hours of event close

### 6. CSAT Survey

**For Student Community Day (SCD):**
- Use the AWS-provided survey: **s12d.com/aws-scd26**
- Display the link / QR code on the closing slide
- Have the MC direct attendees to fill it before leaving

**For re:Invent Watch & Build Party (or non-SCD events):**
- Create a short Google Form (5-7 questions max):
  1. Overall satisfaction (1-5 stars)
  2. "I learned something new today" (Strongly Agree → Strongly Disagree)
  3. "I would recommend this event to a friend" (Yes / No)
  4. What did you enjoy most? (open text)
  5. What could we improve? (open text)
  6. Which session was your favorite? (multiple choice)
  7. Would you attend our next event? (Yes / No / Maybe)

**Day-of execution:**
- Display the survey QR code on the last slide of the closing session
- MC says: "Before you leave, please take 60 seconds to fill this out — it helps us make the next event even better"
- Have volunteers at the exit gently remind people
- Incentive: "Everyone who fills the survey enters a raffle for [small prize]"

### 7. Sponsor Booth Traffic

**Setup:**
- Place a simple **tally counter** (physical clicker or phone app) at each sponsor booth
- Alternatively, put a **sign-in sheet** at each booth (Name, Email, "Interested in [product]? Y/N")

**Day-of execution:**
- Booth volunteer clicks the counter for every person who stops and engages (not just walks by)
- If using sign-in sheets, collect them at event close
- Report the count per booth in the sponsor-specific metrics table
