## Compliance Boundary: Co-Hosting with Competing Cloud Communities

Per AWS Student Builder Group guidelines:

> **Do not allow competing cloud providers to sponsor your meetup (this includes Google and Microsoft).** If co-hosting with a Google Developer Student Club or Azure meetup, consider splitting costs equally.

**Operational Rules for GDG/GDSC Co-Hosting:**

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cost splitting | Split shared costs (venue, catering) equally between both communities |
| AWS branding | AWS logos, banners, and branded materials must remain distinct and unmodified |
| No competing logos on AWS materials | Google/GDG logos must NOT appear on AWS-branded slides, banners, or handouts |
| Separate branding zones | If banners are displayed, keep AWS and Google branding in separate physical areas |
| Session content | GDG speakers may present on cloud-agnostic topics (open source, career skills, general development); they must NOT present Google Cloud-specific content at an AWS-branded event |
| Social media | Posts from the AWS SBG account use only AWS branding; joint promotion can go on a neutral/shared account |

**Recommended Approach:** Invite GDG members as attendees and offer a cloud-agnostic speaking slot (e.g., "Open Source Development," "Career Readiness") rather than a full co-host arrangement. This avoids branding complexity while maintaining the relationship.
