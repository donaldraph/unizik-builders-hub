Who We Are

The AWS Student Builders Group at Nnamdi Azikiwe University (AWS SBG UNIZIK) is the official, student-led cloud computing and builder community on campus in Awka, Anambra State, backed directly by Amazon Web Services (AWS).

We are part of a global network of AWS Student Builder Groups across universities worldwide. AWS funds us with grants, gives our members 12 months of premium Skill Builder access (Cloud Quest, SimuLearn, Builder Labs, Card Clash, AWS Jam), and connects us to a worldwide community of builders.

We don't just talk about tech. We ship it.

Backed by Amazon Web Services with grants, tools, and global community support

AWS re:Invent Watch & Build Party

AWS re:Invent is Amazon's biggest annual cloud computing conference, held in Las Vegas every December. Thousands of developers, engineers, and cloud architects fly in from around the world. New services get launched. Keynotes get wild.

Our Watch & Build Party brings that experience to Awka. We livestream the keynotes, run hands-on build sprints with whatever AWS just dropped, play campus games, and give out prizes. All in one high-energy day.
